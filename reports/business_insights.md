# Olist E-commerce — Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Sales Performance

**Findings**
- Total recorded product sales: R$13.59 million, excluding freight.
- Total orders containing items: 98,666.
- Average Order Value (AOV): R$137.75.
- Sales growth was primarily driven by increasing order volume rather than increasing AOV.
- November 2017 recorded the highest monthly product sales at approximately R$1.01 million.

**Recommendations**
- Explore product bundling and cross-selling opportunities to increase AOV.
- Investigate periods of high sales performance to identify potential seasonal demand patterns.

## 2. Delivery Performance

**Findings**
- Average delivery time: 12.56 days.
- Late delivery rate: 8.11%.
- A total of 7,826 delivered orders arrived after their estimated delivery date.

**Recommendations**
- Monitor late-delivery rates by seller and shipping region.
- Identify recurring logistical bottlenecks.
- Prioritize reducing delays for sellers or delivery routes with consistently poor performance.

## 3. Customer Satisfaction

**Findings**
- Average customer rating for on-time deliveries: 4.29/5.
- Average customer rating for late deliveries: 2.57/5.
- Late deliveries were associated with a 1.72-point lower average review score.

**Recommendations**
- Investigate delivery reliability as a potential driver of customer dissatisfaction.
- Introduce proactive communication when shipment delays are anticipated.
- Evaluate whether delivery improvements lead to measurable increases in customer satisfaction.

## Limitations

- Product sales exclude freight charges and do not represent Olist's net revenue or profit.
- The monthly trend excludes incomplete boundary months to avoid misleading comparisons.
- Ten review records were not imported into MySQL and were excluded from the SQL-based review analysis.
- The relationship between late delivery and lower ratings is observational and does not establish causation.
